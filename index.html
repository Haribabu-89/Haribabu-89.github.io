My Work

Projects

01 ──
HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI
Two-page employee analytics dashboard tracking income, performance ratings, and work-life balance across departments and job roles. Includes KPI cards, a waterfall breakdown of income by role and overtime status, and interactive slicers for drill-down analysis.
Power BI DAX Data Visualization
02 ──
Sales & Profit Dashboard — Tableau
Interactive dashboard analyzing sales and profit across the Indian Superstore dataset. Combines a category-wise bar chart, a dual-axis sales vs. profit trend line, and a state-level filled map, with cross-filtering actions linking category, sub-category, and region.
Tableau Data Visualization Geo Mapping
03 ──
Super Store Sales & Performance Analysis — SQL
25-query SQL project analyzing store-level sales, profit, and order data. Covers aggregate reporting, filtering with pattern matching and range conditions, subqueries (scalar, ALL/ANY, IN), and joins (inner, left, right, self, natural, cross) to answer business questions across region, segment, and category.
SQL MySQL Joins & Subqueries
04 ──
EV Charging Station Analysis — Python
Analyzed 8,300+ EV charging records to uncover high-performing stations, peak demand periods, and the effect of weather on charging behavior. Engineered features like charging efficiency and cost, then used statistical tests and visualizations to surface actionable insights.
Python Pandas Seaborn
05 ──
Real Mart Sales Analysis — Excel
Interactive Excel dashboard tracking total sales, profit, orders, and customer rating for a retail chain. Includes monthly sales trend, sales by city and branch, sales vs. gross income by product line, and payment method breakdown — all filterable by product line, branch, city, and gender.
Excel Pivot Tables Slicers
